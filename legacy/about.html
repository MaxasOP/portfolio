Origin story

I build with structure first, then add the sparkle.

The work I enjoy most sits between frontend execution and product thinking. The goal is simple: make the path readable, the interface confident, and the details worth keeping.

I am currently pursuing MBA Tech in Computer Engineering at NMIMS, Mumbai. I like systems that feel deliberate, not decorative for the sake of it.

My approach is practical: define the hierarchy, remove noise, and let motion reinforce the message instead of fighting it.

That usually leads to work that feels clean on first glance and still makes sense after a closer look.
